A garden that rewards slowing down. At its heart is a natural pond, its
margins thick with irises, grasses and marginal planting that draw dragonflies
and other visitors through the summer.

A green-painted pergola runs along one side, draped in wisteria in late spring
and threaded with clematis as the season turns. The block-paved path beneath it
is flanked by mixed borders — foxgloves and salvias, apricot roses, sea holly
and the bold, silvered leaves of cardoons — layered for colour and structure
from spring right through to autumn.

Stone figures, painted pebbles and quiet places to sit give the planting
somewhere to settle against.
